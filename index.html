Installation Guide

How to Install Vendo on Your Android Phone

Installing an APK directly takes just a few seconds. Follow these 4 simple steps:

1

Download the APK

Tap the Download Vendo for Android button from your Android phone's browser.

2

Open Downloaded File

Once downloaded, tap the download notification in your status bar or find the file in your Downloads folder.

3

Allow Installation

If your phone prompts for permission to install unknown apps, tap Settings and toggle on Allow from this source.

4

Complete & Open

Tap Install. Once installation completes, tap Open to launch Vendo and explore products and services!

ℹ️
Why does Android ask for installation permission?
Android protects devices by default whenever an application is downloaded outside of the Google Play Store. Because Vendo is distributed directly as an APK, your phone simply asks you to confirm the installation.
